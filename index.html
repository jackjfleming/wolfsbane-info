Villagers

Tonight, if it was true, a player dies.townsfolkGrandmotherYou start knowing 1 player & their character (they are Good). (Or that zero are in play.)townsfolkMayorIf only 3 players live & no execution occurs, your team wins. Outsiders

outsiderBillWhen Bill’s ability works at game end, reverse the winner and loser, even if Bill is dead.outsiderButlerEach night choose another player; tomorrow, Butler may vote only when that Master votes.outsiderGoonEach night, the first player who chooses Goon is confused until dusk and Goon takes that player’s alignment.outsiderKlutzWhen Klutz learns they died, they publicly choose one living player; if that player counts as a Werewolf, Klutz’s current team loses.outsiderLunaticYou think you are an Alpha. Your apparent choices do nothing; the real Alpha knows who you are and sees every player you choose each night.outsiderMoonchildWhen you learn you died, publicly choose a living player. If they counted as a Villager then, they die tonight if your ability works and protection does not save them.outsiderRecluseYou might register as evil & as a Minion or Alpha Wolf, even if dead.outsiderSaintIf you die by execution, your current team loses. Bill may invert the result.outsiderTinkerWhile your ability works, the Storyteller may choose to kill you at any time. Protection against death can prevent it; protection limited to Alpha effects cannot.outsiderVillage IdiotYou do not know you are the Village Idiot. You think you are a Townsfolk character, but you are actually an Outsider without that ability.

Minions

[+2 Outsiders]minionEvil TwinYou & an opposing player know each other. If the good player is executed, evil wins. Good can't win if you both live.minionGodfatherYou start knowing which Outsiders are in play. If 1 died today, choose a player tonight: they die. [-1 or +1 Outsider]minionMastermindIf the Alpha dies by execution and that would end the game, play one more day. You might register as good & as a Villager or Outsider, even if dead.minionSuccessorIf at least 5 non-Traveler players are alive immediately before the Alpha dies, you immediately become that Alpha.minionAdvocateEach night, choose a living player (different from last night): if executed tomorrow, they do not die.

Travelers
