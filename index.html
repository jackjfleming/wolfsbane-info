Outsiders

outsiderBillWhoever wins, loses & whoever loses, wins, even if you are dead.outsiderButlerEach night, choose a player (not yourself): tomorrow, you may only vote if they are voting too.outsiderGoonEach night, the first player to choose you with their ability is confused until dusk. You become their alignment.outsiderKlutzWhen you learn that you died, publicly choose 1 alive player: if they are evil, your team loses.outsiderLunaticYou think you are the Alpha, but your abilities malfunction. The Alpha knows who you are & who you attack.outsiderMoonchildWhen you learn you are dead, choose 1 alive player: if they are a Villager, they die tonight.outsiderRecluseYou might register as evil & as a Minion or Alpha Wolf, even if dead.outsiderSaintIf you die by execution, your team loses.outsiderTinkerYou might die at any time.outsiderVillage IdiotYou do not know you are the Village Idiot. You think you are a Villager character, but you are not.

Minions

[+2 Outsiders]minionEvil TwinYou & an opposing player know each other. If the good player is executed, evil wins. Good can't win if you both live.minionGodfatherYou start knowing which Outsiders are in play. If 1 died today, choose a player tonight: they die. [-1 or +1 Outsider]minionMastermindIf the Alpha dies, play for 1 more day. (Travelers don't count.)minionAdvocateEach night, choose a living player (different from last night): if executed tomorrow, they do not die.

Travelers
