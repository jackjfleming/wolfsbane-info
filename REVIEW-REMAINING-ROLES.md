# Remaining storyteller role review

**Status: 21 of these 36 guides approved from these annotations; 15 remain drafts.** The proposed rulings below summarize Wolf's Bane's abilities and current guide decisions. The Blood on the Clocktower column is comparison material, not an automatic Wolf's Bane rule. Each row links to its role guide and an official Clocktower source. The last column records approvals and remaining decisions. [Trapper](roles/trapper.html) stays deferred as requested.

The editable source is [`content/role-reference.json`](content/role-reference.json). Once a role is approved, update its guide and review status, regenerate the bundles, and add any executable gap to [`GAME-MASTER-ACTION-ITEMS.md`](GAME-MASTER-ACTION-ITEMS.md).

## Outsiders

| Role | Proposed ruling | Blood on the Clocktower comparison | Decision to review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Lunatic](roles/lunatic.html) | Lunatic believes they are Alpha; their apparent attacks do nothing, but the real Alpha learns Lunatic's identity and choices. | Official [Lunatic](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Lunatic) is shown apparent Minions and bluffs, chooses before the Demon, and the Demon sees each choice. The apparent Demon can differ from the actual one. | **Approved.** Follow the linked BOTC setup and night procedure. Lunatic may believe they are a different Alpha type, including one that chooses multiple targets; the real Alpha sees every chosen target. |
| [Moonchild](roles/moonchild.html) | On learning of death, publicly choose one living player; if they counted as a Villager when chosen, they die tonight if Moonchild's ability works then. | Official [Moonchild](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Moonchild) checks **good at choice time** but impairment at night resolution; a Goon changing alignment afterward does not change the saved check. | **Approved.** Villager includes good Outsiders and Travelers. Test alignment and discretionary registration when the public choice is made; at night, apply the chosen player’s actual protection to the resulting kill attempt. |
| [Recluse](roles/recluse.html) | May register as Werewolf and as a specific Minion or Alpha for each relevant check, even while dead, without gaining that ability. | Official [Recluse](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse) may register differently across checks, including after death, but remains an actual good Outsider. | **Approved.** Per-check Werewolf, Minion, or Alpha registration can affect target-sensitive abilities as well as information, without changing the actual role or team. |
| [Saint](roles/saint.html) | If Saint dies by execution, Saint's current team loses. A prevented death does not trigger it. | Official [Saint](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Saint) makes good lose when Saint dies by execution; execution without death is insufficient. | **Approved.** The actual Saint’s current team loses if Saint dies by execution; Bill inverts the result once. Registration-only Saint does not trigger this loss. |
| [Tinker](roles/tinker.html) | While the ability works, Storyteller may choose when Tinker dies. General death protection may save them; Alpha-only protection does not protect against Tinker’s own ability. Announce a day death immediately or a night death at dawn. | Official [Tinker](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Tinker) may die at any time, but cannot die from their ability while protected from death; impairment prevents the ability from causing death. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** A successful Tinker death counts as an ordinary death for another ability only when that ability’s own cause and timing conditions are met. For example, a daytime Tinker death blocks Zombuul’s next attack. Owner approval of this last interaction remains pending. |
| [Village Idiot](roles/village-idiot.html) | Is an actual Outsider who thinks they are a Townsfolk. Their apparent ability malfunctions; an attempted once-per-game use appears spent. Other roles see Village Idiot as the actual Outsider. | The analogous [Drunk](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Drunk) thinks they are a Townsfolk but is actually an Outsider with no Townsfolk ability. | **Approved.** Perceived character must be Townsfolk; apparent once-per-game attempts look spent and fail; other roles check the actual Village Idiot Outsider, absent a separate registration effect. |

## Minions

| Role | Proposed ruling | Blood on the Clocktower comparison | Decision to review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Assassin](roles/assassin.html) | Once per game after the first night, choose a player to die, bypassing ordinary death protection; impairment prevents the kill but spends the use. | Official [Assassin](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Assassin) bypasses protection, including Fool and Goon; a drunk or poisoned Assassin has no effective kill. | **Approved.** Bypasses ordinary cannot-die effects; a choice spends the use while already impaired. Preserve the approved Goon interaction when choosing Goon causes confusion. |
| [Baron](roles/baron.html) | At setup replace two Townsfolk with Outsiders; the setup change persists after Baron's death. | Official [Baron](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Baron) adds two Outsiders in place of Townsfolk and has a [Heretic](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Heretic) incompatibility, now adopted for Bill. | **Approved.** Replace two Townsfolk with available Outsiders at setup; resolve a script without enough eligible Outsiders before play. Bill and Baron cannot coexist. |
| [Evil Twin](roles/evil-twin.html) | Pair with an opposing-alignment player; if that player is executed, Werewolves win, and Villagers cannot win while both twins live. | Official [Evil Twin](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Evil_Twin) checks which paired player is currently **good**, regardless of their character token. If both twins become the same alignment, Storyteller chooses a new opposite-team twin. Executing the good-aligned twin can win for evil without that twin dying; a dead Evil Twin normally loses the ability. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** “Alignment” means a paired player’s current team, regardless of character name. If both twins become the same team, choose a new opposite-team twin. Executing the currently Villager-aligned twin causes the loss even if death is prevented; Bill inverts once. Owner approval remains pending. |
| [Godfather](roles/godfather.html) | At setup add or remove one Outsider; learn Outsiders in play. If an Outsider died during the day, choose one night victim. | Official [Godfather](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Godfather) gets one kill even if multiple Outsiders died that day; night deaths do not count. It is incompatible with Heretic, hence Bill. | **Approved.** Day deaths, including execution or exile, can trigger one night kill; use discretionary Outsider registration, choose the setup modifier, and enforce Bill exclusion. |
| [Mastermind](roles/mastermind.html) | If the Alpha dies by execution and that would end the game, play one more day. A good player's execution wins for Werewolves; executing evil or nobody wins for Villagers. | Official [Mastermind](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Mastermind) uses this trigger and outcome. It differs materially from Wolf's Bane's former printed text. | **Approved.** Use the linked BOTC trigger and extra-day outcomes, subject to Bill inversion. |
| [Poisoner](roles/poisoner.html) | Each night choose any player; they have no working ability through the following day, then recover at dusk unless another effect remains. | Official [Poisoner](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Poisoner) poisons through the next day; a poisoned player still appears to wake and act, and an attempted once-per-game use is spent. | **Approved.** A once-per-game attempt while poisoned is spent. Each poison source keeps its own expiry, so overlapping sources continue until all have ended. |
| [Spy](roles/spy.html) | See the Grimoire nightly; may register as Villager and a particular Townsfolk or Outsider, even while dead, without gaining that ability. | Official [Spy](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spy) can register as good and a specific Townsfolk/Outsider differently for each check; it is incompatible with Heretic, hence Bill. | **Approved.** Registration can affect target-sensitive abilities and is chosen per check. Show the current Grimoire; enforce Bill exclusion. |
| [Successor](roles/successor.html) | If at least five non-Traveler players are alive just before Alpha dies, Successor immediately becomes that Alpha. A Fenrir self-kill passes to Successor. | Analogous [Scarlet Woman](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scarlet_Woman) converts **immediately** when the Demon dies with five or more non-Traveler players alive just before death. | **Approved.** At least five living non-Travelers immediately before Alpha death. Convert immediately; eligible Successor takes Fenrir self-kill before another Minion, but a Fang Gu jump goes to the attacked Outsider and leaves Successor unchanged. |
| [Advocate](roles/advocate.html) | Each night choose a living player, including self but not last night's choice; tomorrow that player survives execution, though execution still occurs. | Official [Devil's Advocate](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Devil%27s_Advocate) cannot repeat a target on consecutive nights, can self-protect, and prevents execution death while execution succeeds. | **Approved.** Self-choice is allowed; consecutive-night restriction applies even after no execution. A protected Zombuul that appears dead is still living; impairment pauses protection. |

## Alpha Wolves

| Role | Proposed ruling | Blood on the Clocktower comparison | Decision to review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Fang Gu](roles/fang-gu.html) | Add one Outsider at setup. On the first qualifying Outsider attack, that Outsider becomes a Werewolf Fang Gu and the original Fang Gu dies instead; later Outsider attacks are ordinary kills. | Official [Fang Gu](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fang_Gu) jumps only if its attack would kill an Outsider, once per game; a protected Outsider does not trigger the jump, and the Outsider survives as the new Fang Gu. | **BOTC answers proposed; draft.** A jump occurs on the first attack that would kill an Outsider; protection prevents it. The once-per-game jump belongs to the game, not each Fang Gu. Inform the new Fang Gu immediately; they do not attack again that night or receive Minion identities. Apply approved registration per check. **Owner confirmed** the attacked Outsider takes the jump ahead of Successor. |
| [Fenrir](roles/alpha-wolf.html) | After the first night kill one player; if Fenrir kills themself this way, a living Minion becomes Fenrir. | Analogous [Imp](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Imp) self-kill promotes an alive Minion immediately; the new Demon does not attack again that night. | **Approved.** On a successful self-kill, eligible Successor takes priority; otherwise Storyteller chooses a living Minion. Promotion is immediate, with no second attack that night. If nobody is eligible, Villagers win subject to Bill and other active win rules. |
| [No Dashii](roles/no-dashii.html) | After the first night kill a player; the two nearest Townsfolk in opposite seating directions are poisoned while the ability works. | Official [No Dashii](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/No_Dashii) skips non-Townsfolk but **not dead Townsfolk**; poisoned neighbors change immediately after seat or character changes. | **Approved: follow BOTC.** Dead Townsfolk count; actual Village Idiot Outsider is skipped; apply discretionary Spy registration. Recalculate after seat/character changes. No Dashii poison stops while its source has no ability. |
| [Po](roles/po.html) | After the first night kill one player, or choose nobody to make three ordered choices the following night. | Official [Po](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Po) charges by choosing no one, even if impaired; a failed kill is not a charge, and the three choices are resolved in order. | **Approved: follow BOTC.** Three choices are required on the next acting night after choosing nobody, and resolve in order. Choosing nobody while impaired still charges; a failed attack does not. A missed wake preserves the charge. Normal choice rules govern repeated targets. |
| [Pukka](roles/pukka.html) | Act on night one: poison a chosen player now; the previous target dies on the next night and then recovers. | Official [Pukka](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Pukka) poisons immediately; its earlier target dies after the next choice. If Pukka loses its ability, an existing effect pauses and may resume. | **Approved: follow BOTC.** Poison new target first, then attempt prior target’s death while still poisoned, then end only Pukka’s poison on that target. Death protection may stop the kill; source impairment pauses the old effect, while a skipped wake alone need not. |
| [Shabaloth](roles/shabaloth.html) | After the first night choose two players to die in order; Storyteller may revive a chosen player from the previous night. | Official [Shabaloth](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Shabaloth) may regurgitate even a previously dead player it attacked; revived players regain abilities, including used first-night and once-per-game abilities. | **Approved: follow BOTC.** Choose two targets in order, including dead targets. Before the next Shabaloth wake, Storyteller may revive one dead player chosen last night. Revived abilities reset; first-night-only ability acts that night, and ordinary wake order still applies. |
| [Vigormortis](roles/vigormortis.html) | After the first night kill one player; Minions killed this way keep their ability and poison one nearest Townsfolk; **remove one Outsider**. | Official [Vigormortis](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Vigormortis) has **−1 Outsider** and each killed Minion poisons a neighboring Townsfolk; it does not give Villager registration. | **Approved: replace Wolf’s Bane draft with BOTC.** Setup is **−1 Outsider**; each Minion killed by Vigormortis retains ability and poisons one nearest Townsfolk chosen by Storyteller. No Villager registration. Apply the official Mastermind jinx when Vigormortis dies. |
| [Zombuul](roles/zombuul.html) | If nobody died today, attack after the first night. First would-be death makes Zombuul appear dead but secretly remain alive; second kills it. | Official [Zombuul](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Zombuul) counts an apparent first death as death for most public rules, but the Demon remains alive for its ability and victory. A dead player's execution without death still allows a night attack. | **Approved: follow BOTC.** Apparent first death counts as a public death; while apparently dead, Zombuul uses dead voting, nomination, and neighbor rules but remains alive for its Alpha ability and victory. If impaired before first death, it dies for real. |

## Travelers

| Role | Proposed ruling | Blood on the Clocktower comparison | Decision to review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Apprentice](roles/apprentice.html) | On their first night a Villager Apprentice gains a Townsfolk ability, or a Werewolf Apprentice gains a Minion ability, without changing character. | Official [Apprentice](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Apprentice) gains an ability from the character sheet, may use it that same night, and remains a Traveler for character detection/exile. | **Approved: follow BOTC.** Storyteller chooses an on-sheet Townsfolk or Minion ability by Apprentice alignment. First-night abilities work on Apprentice’s own first night; duplicates are possible; Apprentice remains a Traveler. Later alignment change does not grant another ability. |
| [Barista](roles/barista.html) | Each night Barista chooses a player; Storyteller grants true information or makes the ability work twice tonight/tomorrow. | Official [Barista](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Barista) has the **Storyteller choose both recipient and effect** until dusk; true-info mode also makes the recipient sober and healthy. Used once-per-game abilities may work twice. | **BOTC answer available; design decision remains.** Officially Storyteller chooses both recipient and effect, tells the recipient, and true-information mode also makes them sober/healthy. A spent once-per-game ability may gain one more use. Confirm adopting this instead of Wolf’s Bane’s player-chosen recipient. |
| [Beggar](roles/beggar.html) | Needs a vote token to vote; a dead player may give theirs, after which Beggar learns that player's alignment. Beggar remains sober and healthy. | Official [Beggar](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Beggar) spends one token per vote, can vote on exile without one, and cannot become drunk or poisoned. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** Token donation is voluntary; learn donor alignment privately with registration discretion. Spend one token per execution vote; exile votes need none. On death, discard acquired tokens and receive the usual one dead vote. |
| [Bone Collector](roles/bone-collector.html) | Once per game at night choose a dead player; restore their ability temporarily while they remain dead. | Official [Bone Collector](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bone_Collector) restores ability **until dusk**, including already-used once-per-game/first-night abilities, and it ends if Bone Collector dies. Wolf's Bane text says “until dawn tomorrow.” | **BOTC answer available; design decision remains.** Official effect lasts until **dusk**, restores spent and first-night abilities while recipient remains dead, and ends if Bone Collector dies. Confirm replacing Wolf’s Bane’s printed “dawn tomorrow” expiry. |
| [Bureaucrat](roles/bureaucrat.html) | Each night choose another player; their execution vote counts as three tomorrow while Bureaucrat's ability works. | Official [Bureaucrat](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bureaucrat) applies to every execution vote that day, ends immediately if Bureaucrat dies, and does not affect exile votes. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** Repeat nightly targets are allowed, but not self; dead targets are permitted. All target execution votes that day count as three while the source works. Death/impairment ends effect; exile votes unaffected. With Thief on same voter, total is −3. |
| [Butcher](roles/butcher.html) | After the first execution each day, Butcher may nominate once more, potentially causing a second execution. | Official [Butcher](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butcher) may nominate even if first execution did not kill; the second nomination needs half of living players, not a higher vote than the first, and cannot chain to a third. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** A survived first execution still permits Butcher’s second nomination, including a repeat nominee. Its vote needs the normal half-of-living threshold, and no third nomination follows from this ability. Mayor’s no-execution condition has already failed. |
| [Gangster](roles/gangster.html) | Once per day kill one nearest living neighbor if the other nearest living neighbor agrees in Storyteller's presence. | Official [Gangster](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gangster) skips dead seats; an agreed attempt uses the daily ability even if protection prevents death. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** Use nearest living neighbors after seat changes, with agreement in Storyteller’s presence; an agreed attempt is spent even if death is prevented. The official page is unclear on retry if the other neighbor refuses, so that point remains open. |
| [Gunslinger](roles/gunslinger.html) | Immediately after the first execution vote is tallied, optionally kill one player who voted. | Official [Gunslinger](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gunslinger) gets one opportunity after that first vote, cannot wait for later votes, and exile votes do not qualify. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** The first execution vote offers the only shot, even if tied or failed. Choose an actual voter immediately; ordinary protection applies. Exile votes do not trigger it. |
| [Judge](roles/judge.html) | Once per game, during or just after a nomination, force that nomination's execution to pass or fail. | Official [Judge](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Judge) can act only on **another player's** current nomination. A forced pass immediately executes, subject to a Butcher follow-up; a forced fail cancels its votes. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** Judge may act on a nomination **made by someone else**, through the end of its tally but before the next nomination. Forced pass executes immediately, subject to survival and Butcher; forced fail cancels only that nomination. |
| [Matron](roles/matron.html) | Each day choose which two players sit next to Matron; update seating-dependent abilities from that arrangement. | Official [Matron](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Matron) may instead make up to **three pairs of seat swaps** per day and prevents leaving seats for private game talks. | **BOTC answer available; design decision remains.** Officially Matron may make up to three consecutive pair swaps in one daily opportunity; seating persists, and private game talks away from seats are barred subject to accessibility. Confirm replacing Wolf’s Bane’s two-neighbor power. |
| [Scapegoat](roles/scapegoat.html) | When a same-alignment player would be executed, Storyteller may execute Scapegoat instead. | Official [Scapegoat](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scapegoat) is an optional replacement **execution**, not just a redirected death; it ends nominations and may itself fail to kill if protected. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** Storyteller may replace the execution itself while Scapegoat is living and counts as the same team, including via Spy/Recluse registration. A saved Saint was not executed. Resolve New in Town’s separate nomination trigger before applying this execution replacement. |
| [Thief](roles/thief.html) | Each night choose another player; their execution vote counts as −1 tomorrow while Thief's ability works. | Official [Thief](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Thief) makes each affected vote subtract one, ends if Thief dies, and does not affect exile voting. | **BOTC answer proposed; draft.** Repeat targets allowed, but not self; each affected execution vote subtracts one while Thief works. Death/impairment ends effect, exile votes unaffected, and combined Bureaucrat/Thief vote is −3. |
