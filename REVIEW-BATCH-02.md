# Storyteller role guide review — batch 2

Status: **Minstrel, New in Town, Pacifist, Professor, and Ravenkeeper approved by the Wolf’s Bane owner on 2026-09-27.**

| Role | Approved ruling |
| --- | --- |
| [Minstrel](roles/minstrel.html) | An actual Minion death by execution confuses everyone except Minstrel and Travelers until the following dusk. |
| [New in Town](roles/new-in-town.html) | The first nomination spends the ability; a qualifying Townsfolk nominator is immediately executed. |
| [Pacifist](roles/pacifist.html) | Any executed good player, including a good Outsider, may survive at the Storyteller’s discretion. |
| [Professor](roles/professor.html) | Choose any dead player once; only a qualifying Townsfolk is resurrected. |
| [Ravenkeeper](roles/ravenkeeper.html) | A night death triggers the choice, including a first-night death if one occurs. |

The owner clarified that a good Outsider is a Villager for Pacifist. Professor resurrection is limited to Townsfolk. Ravenkeeper's usual absence on the first night follows the usual Alpha attack timing, not a restriction on Ravenkeeper's ability.

Comparison rules: [Minstrel](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Minstrel), [Virgin](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Virgin), [Pacifist](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Pacifist), [Professor](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Professor), and [Ravenkeeper](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ravenkeeper).

The publication gate still requires approval of every selectable role.
