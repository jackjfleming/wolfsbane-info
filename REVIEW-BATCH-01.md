## Decisions recorded

1. **Courtier:** Use “poisoned” terminology. A poisoned choice spends the use without effect. Poisoning the Courtier during an active effect suspends it; recovery before expiry resumes it. The three-night clock keeps running. This follows the [comparison game's Courtier impairment ruling](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Courtier).
2. **Gambler:** Allow self-choice and judge the target's current character. When a healthy Spy or Recluse could register as the guessed character, the Storyteller chooses that registration for this check. See the [Gambler](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gambler), [Spy](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spy), and [Recluse](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse) comparison rules.
3. **Other exceptional cases:** The owner approved the Chambermaid, Clockmaker, and Fool guidance as drafted.
