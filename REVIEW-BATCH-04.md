# Storyteller role guide review — batch 4

Status: **Draft for owner review.** Trapper, Bill, Butler, Goon, and Klutz remain unapproved. The previous 29 approvals are preserved.

| Role | Proposed ruling | Blood on the Clocktower comparison | Decision to review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Trapper](roles/trapper.html) | After the first night, protect one chosen player from the Alpha and learn whether the target is the Alpha. | There is no direct official equivalent. The [Monk](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Monk) chooses another player who is safe from the Demon; protecting the Demon does not shut off all its actions. Monk cannot choose themself and has no detection ability, so its rules do not settle Trapper's target restrictions or feedback. The [Recluse](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse) can register as a Demon, but applying that to Trapper's custom detection still needs a Wolf's Bane decision. | Should this follow the printed protection-plus-detection ability, or Game Master's current behavior that blocks all Alpha actions when Trapper picks the Alpha? Are self, dead, and repeat targets allowed? Can Recluse registration affect detection, and should a non-Alpha target receive an explicit no? |
| [Bill](roles/bill.html) | At game end, reverse the normal winner once if Bill's ability works, even when Bill is dead. | The analogous [Heretic](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Heretic) reverses victory conditions even while dead, unless drunk or poisoned. Its official page lists setup jinxes that prevent certain character combinations; it does not give a general rule for multiple Heretics. Those jinxes and any rule for multiple Bills need an explicit Wolf's Bane decision. | Should Heretic-style setup incompatibilities apply? How do multiple Bill abilities interact? |
| [Butler](roles/butler.html) | Choose another player as Master nightly; tomorrow Butler may vote only if that Master votes. Dead Master and free exile voting are allowed. | Official [Butler](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butler) may choose a living or dead Master and vote when that Master's hand is up or their vote has already been counted. An accidental illegal vote is still tallied, then handled privately. Butler may vote freely on exile because character abilities do not affect it. The official ability has no setup modifier. | Confirm counting an accidental illegal vote. Butler has no printed setup effect, but Game Master currently changes the role mix when selected. |
| [Goon](roles/goon.html) | First player to choose Goon each night becomes confused until dusk; Goon copies their current alignment. | Official [Goon](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Goon) makes the first chooser each night drunk immediately until dusk and takes that chooser's alignment; later choices work normally. Its examples say Assassin still kills Goon despite becoming drunk, and show that a multi-target ability's outcome depends on whether it reaches Goon before or after another target. Wolf's Bane uses “confused” for the analogous impairment. | Confirm Assassin's bypass and sequential multi-target resolution. |
| [Klutz](roles/klutz.html) | On learning of death, publicly choose one living player; if that player is evil, Klutz's current team loses. | Official [Klutz](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Klutz) checks whether the publicly chosen living player is **evil**, rather than whether they have a particular character. An evil Klutz can cause the evil team to lose. The Klutz page does not address registration, though [Spy](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spy) and [Recluse](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse) can register with a different alignment under their own abilities; applying that here is an inference requiring a ruling. | Does “Werewolf” mean current evil alignment, including evil Goon and Travelers? Can Spy/Recluse registration change the check? |

Game Master discrepancies are listed in the editorial notes on each draft page. Approval of these rulings does not itself change executable rules.

Trapper is drafted from Wolf's Bane's printed ability and app behavior. The linked Clocktower rules are comparisons; decisions for Wolf's Bane's custom roles remain open until approved.
