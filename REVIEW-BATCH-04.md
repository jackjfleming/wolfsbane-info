# Storyteller role guide review — batch 4

Status: **Bill, Butler, Goon, and Klutz approved by the Wolf’s Bane owner on 2026-09-27.** Trapper was approved later on 2026-09-28 with an Exorcist-style replacement ability. The previous 29 approvals are preserved.

| Role | Wolf’s Bane ruling | Blood on the Clocktower comparison | Review status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Trapper](roles/trapper.html) | After night one, choose someone different from last night. If the chosen player is the Alpha, they learn Trapper’s identity and skip their ordinary wake; Trapper learns nothing directly. | [Exorcist](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Exorcist) supplies the adopted rule. [Monk](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Monk) corresponds to Wolf’s Bane’s Bodyguard. | **Approved 2026-09-28.** Replaces the printed protection and detection ability; Warden already has a similar ability. Game Master work is GM-43. |
| [Bill](roles/bill.html) | Reverse the normal winner once when Bill’s ability works, even if Bill is dead. Allow only one Bill ability. Bill cannot coexist with Baron, Godfather, or Spy. Apply the corresponding Heretic jinxes if Boffin, Lleech, Pit-Hag, or Widow are added later. | [Heretic](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Heretic) works while dead unless impaired. Its jinxes say only one of Heretic and Baron, Godfather, Lleech, Pit-Hag, Spy, or Widow can be in play; Boffin’s Demon cannot have the Heretic ability. | **Approved.** Multiple Bill abilities are forbidden, including through role changes or ability copies. |
| [Butler](roles/butler.html) | Choose another player as Master nightly; tomorrow Butler may vote only if that Master votes. Dead Master, counting an accidental illegal vote, and free exile voting are allowed. Butler has no setup modifier. | Official [Butler](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butler) may choose a living or dead Master and vote when that Master's hand is up or their vote has already been counted. An accidental illegal vote is still tallied, then handled privately. Butler may vote freely on exile because character abilities do not affect it. The official ability has no setup modifier. | **Approved.** Game Master's current setup modifier needs a separate app correction. |
| [Goon](roles/goon.html) | First player to choose Goon each night becomes confused until dusk; Goon copies their current alignment. Assassin’s kill still works. Earlier targets of a sequential ability retain resolved effects; later targets use the new confusion. | Official [Goon](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Goon) makes the first chooser each night drunk immediately until dusk and takes that chooser's alignment; later choices work normally. Its examples say Assassin still kills Goon despite becoming drunk, and show that a multi-target ability's outcome depends on whether it reaches Goon before or after another target. Wolf's Bane uses “confused” for the analogous impairment. | **Approved.** Game Master's target-order and kill-immunity behavior needs a separate app review. |
| [Klutz](roles/klutz.html) | On learning of death, publicly choose one living player; if that player counts as a **Werewolf**, Klutz’s current team loses. Current team includes a Werewolf Goon or Traveler. Spy and Recluse registration can change the check at Storyteller discretion. | Official [Klutz](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Klutz) uses the word **evil**. Wolf's Bane uses **Werewolf** in its printed ability. The Klutz page does not address registration, but [Spy](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spy) and [Recluse](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Recluse) may register with a different alignment under their own abilities. | **Approved** with Wolf’s Bane wording and discretionary registration. |

Game Master discrepancies are noted above. Approval of these rulings does not itself change executable rules. The publication gate still requires approval of every selectable role.

Trapper’s later approval supersedes its deferred decision in this batch. The Game Master implementation remains tracked as GM-43.
