townsfolk

Professor

Once per game after the first night, choose a dead player; resurrect them only if they qualify as Townsfolk.

Storyteller procedure

Each night after the first, wake an unused Professor. They may decline or choose any dead player. Declining preserves the use. A choice spends the ability, even if Professor is poisoned or the target does not qualify. If Professor has a working ability and the target is a Townsfolk (or registers as one for this check), remove the target’s death marker and restore them to life. They regain their ability, including a previously used once-per-game ability. If they would wake later this night, wake them then; if their ability is first-night-only, wake them immediately after Professor. At dawn announce the resurrection, without naming its cause.

Key interactions

A good Outsider is a Villager but is not normally a Townsfolk, so Professor does not resurrect them. A qualifying registration can change the result.

A failed choice still spends Professor’s once-per-game use; declining does not.

A revived Townsfolk regains their ability. Resolve their wake in the current night order as appropriate.

Examples

Professor chooses a dead Fool. Fool returns to life and regains their unused or spent first-death protection.

Professor chooses a dead good Outsider. Nothing happens, and Professor’s use is spent.

Professor declines on night two and may choose on a later night.

Tips & tricks

You may wait to use the ability, but a choice spends it even when nobody returns.

A successful resurrection shows that the chosen player qualified as Townsfolk for this check; registration can complicate what that proves about their actual character or team.

A failed resurrection does not prove the target is evil. They may be a good Outsider, the Professor may be poisoned, or registration may affect the result.

Revived Townsfolk regain their abilities, including spent once-per-game abilities and first-night information.
