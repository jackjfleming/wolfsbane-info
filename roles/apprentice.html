Apprentice

On your first night, Storyteller gives you a Townsfolk ability if Villager or a Minion ability if Werewolf; you remain a Traveler.

Storyteller procedure

On the first night after Apprentice enters play, Storyteller chooses and shows one ability from an eligible Townsfolk character if Apprentice is a Villager, or an eligible Minion character if Apprentice is a Werewolf. Apprentice gains that ability without becoming the character and may use it that night if its normal wake time occurs then. A first-night-only ability works on Apprentice’s own first night. A duplicate ability is possible, although a not-in-play character is usually simpler. If Apprentice changes alignment later, the already-gained ability remains; there is no second first-night grant. This follows from the ability being granted only on Apprentice’s first night. Apprentice remains a Traveler for character checks, exile, and player counts.

Key interactions

The gained ability follows its normal limits and can be impaired independently of the fact that Apprentice remains a Traveler.

Another player checking Apprentice’s character sees Apprentice, not the character whose ability was gained.

Examples

A Werewolf Apprentice gains Assassin. They remain Apprentice but may use the Assassin ability according to its once-per-game timing.
