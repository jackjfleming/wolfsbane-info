alpha

Po

After the first night, choose one player to attack or choose nobody to charge; after a no-one choice, you must choose three players on your next acting night.

Storyteller procedure

Each night except the first, wake Po. Normally Po may choose one player or explicitly choose nobody. Choosing nobody charges three attacks, even if Po is impaired; choosing a player whose death fails does not charge. On the next night Po acts, require three choices one at a time and resolve each in order before the next, including any Goon effect. Follow normal choice rules for repeated targets; a later attack on someone already dead has no further death effect. If Po misses a wake after charging, retain the charge until Po next acts. The first night never counts as choosing nobody.

Key interactions

Impairment may stop kills but does not erase an explicit no-one choice or a pending charge.

Sequential resolution matters: if the second target is Goon, confusion may block the third attack.

Examples

Po chooses nobody while poisoned. On the next acting night Po must choose three players, though poisoning then may make the attacks fail.
