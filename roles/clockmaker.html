Clockmaker

On the first night, give the shortest seat distance from the Alpha Wolf to any Minion.

Storyteller procedure

On the first night, find the Alpha Wolf and each Minion in seating order. For each Minion, count one step per neighboring seat along the shorter path around the circle; adjacent players are one step apart. Use the smallest of those distances. Wake the Clockmaker, show that number, and put them to sleep. Game Master calculates a suggested distance from its current seating order; verify the seats and any special registrations before entering the result.

Key interactions

Count seats, not only living players. A Traveler occupying a seat adds a step even though that Traveler is not a Minion.

If the Clockmaker is poisoned or confused when receiving the information, the number may be false.

The information describes the first-night arrangement; later deaths or seat changes do not update it.

Examples

A Minion sits next to the Alpha Wolf. Show 1, even if another Minion is farther away.

The nearest Minion is two seats clockwise from the Alpha Wolf and four seats counterclockwise. Show 2.
