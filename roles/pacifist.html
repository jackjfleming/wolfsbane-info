Pacifist

When a good player is executed, the Storyteller may let them live; the execution still counts.

Storyteller procedure

When a good player, including a good Outsider or the Pacifist, is executed while a living Pacifist has their ability, choose whether that player dies. If saved, announce that the player was executed but did not die, without naming the cause. The execution still ends nominations for the day. Pacifist does not prevent death by exile or other causes. If Pacifist is poisoned, dead, or otherwise without their ability, do not offer this protection.

Key interactions

A good Outsider is a Villager for Pacifist and can be saved. An evil player is not protected.

Pacifist can save themself if their ability is working when they are executed.

Other death protection may prevent an execution death independently; do not attribute survival publicly.

Examples

A good Outsider is executed. The Storyteller chooses to save them; the player remains alive and no further nomination occurs today.

Pacifist is executed while healthy. The Storyteller chooses to let Pacifist live.

A good Traveler is exiled. Pacifist does not protect them from exile.
