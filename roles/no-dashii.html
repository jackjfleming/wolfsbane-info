No Dashii

Your nearest Townsfolk in each seating direction are poisoned, including dead Townsfolk, while your ability works.

Storyteller procedure

On the first night, find the nearest player who counts as Townsfolk in each seating direction from No Dashii, skipping Outsiders, Minions, and Travelers but not dead Townsfolk. Poison those two players while No Dashii has its ability. Recalculate immediately when seats, characters, or relevant registration changes; remove this poison from former neighbors and apply it to new ones. If No Dashii dies or loses its ability, its poison pauses or ends as appropriate. Each night except the first, wake No Dashii to choose a player to attack.

Key interactions

Village Idiot is an actual Outsider and is skipped; Spy may count as Townsfolk for a particular check under the approved registration rule.

If No Dashii is impaired, its poison does not operate; other poison sources remain active.

Examples

A dead Townsfolk is the closest Townsfolk clockwise from No Dashii and remains poisoned.
