Butler

Each night choose another player; tomorrow, Butler may vote only when that Master votes.

Storyteller procedure

Each night, wake Butler and ask them to choose any other player, living or dead. Mark that player as Master for the next day; repeating a previous choice is allowed. During an execution vote, Butler may vote if the Master has their hand raised or the Master’s vote has already been counted. Butler need not vote when Master does. If Master lowers their hand before their vote counts, Butler must lower theirs. If Butler accidentally casts an illegal vote, count it publicly and correct the mistake privately afterward. A dead Butler has no restriction and may use their remaining vote freely. Exile votes are unaffected by Butler’s ability.

Key interactions

A dead Master can enable Butler’s vote only when that Master uses their remaining vote.

Voting position around the circle does not matter once the Master’s vote has been counted.

Butler has no printed setup effect; Game Master currently changes the role mix when Butler is selected, which needs separate correction.

Examples

Butler chooses a dead player as Master. If the dead Master has a vote token and raises their hand, Butler may vote on that nomination, even if Butler is counted first.

Master’s vote is counted before Butler’s seat comes up. Butler may then vote even if Master lowers their hand.

Butler accidentally votes while Master abstains. Count the vote, then explain the rule privately.

Tips & tricks

Choose a Master whose voting judgment you trust, while remembering you are never required to vote with them.

A dead Master may leave you unable to vote for many nominations because they have only one remaining vote.
