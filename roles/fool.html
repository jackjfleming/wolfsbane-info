Fool

Prevent the Fool's first death and mark the ability used; the next death happens normally.

Storyteller procedure

When an alive Fool would die from an execution or a night effect, first resolve any other protection that prevents the death. If death would still happen and the Fool is healthy with an unused ability, leave the Fool alive and mark 'Fool Used'. Do not announce the reason the death failed. A later death proceeds normally. Game Master checks the poisoned state and the 'Fool Used' tag during execution and night resolution.

Key interactions

If another protection stops the death before the Fool ability is needed, leave 'Fool Used' unmarked.

A poisoned or confused Fool cannot use this protection and may die on the first attempt.

Protection and special effects that bypass ordinary death prevention should be resolved before relying on the Fool.

Examples

The healthy, unused Fool is executed with no other protection. They survive; mark 'Fool Used'. A later lethal attack kills them.

A protected Fool is attacked but the earlier protection prevents death. The Fool stays alive with their ability unused.
