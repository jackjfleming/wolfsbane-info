Goon

Each night, the first player who chooses Goon is confused until dusk and Goon takes that player’s alignment.

Storyteller procedure

Each night, track the first choice of Goon that successfully triggers Goon’s working ability. Immediately mark that choosing player confused until the following dusk, then set Goon’s alignment to match that player’s current alignment. Privately tell Goon if their alignment changed. Resolve that player’s ability with the new confusion in effect. Later choices of Goon that night do not trigger Goon again and resolve normally. A Storyteller choosing Goon for another ability does not count as a player choice. Clear the confusion at dusk; reset the first-choice marker for the next night.

Key interactions

An already poisoned or confused player who chooses Goon can still trigger a working Goon; Goon copies that player’s alignment.

Assassin may kill Goon despite becoming confused from the choice.

For an ability with sequential targets, effects already resolved before Goon was chosen remain; resolve later targets with the new confusion in effect.

Goon’s current alignment controls team membership, including after Goon dies and can no longer switch.

A choice made while Goon is impaired does not consume the first successful trigger; if Goon recovers later that night, a later choice can trigger it.

Examples

Poisoner chooses Goon first tonight. Poisoner becomes confused, their poison does not take effect, and Goon becomes evil.

A good Sailor chooses Goon first. Sailor becomes confused and Goon becomes good.

A second player chooses Goon later the same night. Goon’s first-choice effect does not trigger again.
