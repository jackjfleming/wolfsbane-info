Vigormortis

Minions killed by your attack keep their abilities while you live and each poisons one nearest Townsfolk neighbor. Remove one Outsider at setup.

Storyteller procedure

At setup remove one Outsider and add one Townsfolk; if there is no Outsider to remove, make no swap. Each night except the first, wake Vigormortis to attack one player. A Minion killed by this attack remains dead but keeps their ability while Vigormortis lives and has its ability. For each such Minion, Storyteller chooses one nearest Townsfolk clockwise or counterclockwise, skipping other character types but not dead Townsfolk, to poison. Recalculate the affected Townsfolk when relevant characters or seats change. If Vigormortis dies or loses its ability, the retained Minion abilities and poison ordinarily stop; the official Mastermind jinx preserves a Mastermind ability already granted by Vigormortis when Vigormortis dies.

Key interactions

Killed Minions do not register as Villagers; the prior Wolf’s Bane draft wording is superseded by the adopted BOTC rule.

Dead Minions can themselves be poisoned or confused, pausing the retained ability.

Mastermind keeps its granted ability after Vigormortis dies under the official jinx.

Examples

Vigormortis kills Poisoner. Poisoner remains dead but can act, and one nearest Townsfolk is poisoned while Vigormortis continues to empower that Minion.
