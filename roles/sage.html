Sage

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

If the Alpha’s ability kills Sage at night, show two distinct players, including the Alpha who killed them.

Storyteller procedure

When Sage dies from the Alpha’s ability, wake Sage that night. Point to two distinct players, one of whom is the Alpha who caused that death. The other may be alive or dead. Do not wake Sage for execution or a night death caused by another character. If Sage is impaired when the information is given, the two players shown need not include the killer. The Storyteller resolves any special registration before showing the pair.

Key interactions

Track the Alpha who actually caused the death, even if another player later becomes the Alpha.

A night death from Assassin, Gossip, or another non-Alpha ability does not trigger Sage.

The comparison game permits Recluse to register as the Alpha for this information; confirm whether Wolf’s Bane follows that interaction.

Examples

Fenrir kills Sage. The Storyteller shows Fenrir and one other player.

Assassin kills Sage during the night. Sage receives no information.

Sage is poisoned when Fenrir kills them. Any pair shown is unreliable.

Editorial review

Confirm the Recluse registration interaction and first-night behavior if a special Alpha ability kills Sage then.

Game Master currently wakes Sage for any Died Tonight tag; it does not track whether the Alpha caused the death.
