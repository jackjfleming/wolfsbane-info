Sage

If the Alpha’s ability kills Sage, show two players including the Alpha who caused that death.

Storyteller procedure

When Sage dies from the Alpha’s ability, even on the first night if such a kill occurs, wake Sage that night. If Sage’s ability is working, point to two distinct players: the actual Alpha who killed Sage and one other player, who may be alive or dead. Recluse may register as an Alpha for this interaction, but cannot replace the actual killer in the pair. Do not wake Sage for execution or a night death caused by another character. If Sage is impaired when the information is given, a simulated pair may be unreliable.

Key interactions

The pair must include the actual Alpha who caused Sage’s death; a later replacement Alpha cannot stand in for that killer.

Recluse may register as an Alpha at Storyteller discretion, but the actual killer still appears in a working Sage result.

An Alpha kill on the first night triggers Sage; death from Assassin, Gossip, or another non-Alpha ability does not.

Examples

Fenrir kills Sage. The Storyteller shows Fenrir and one other player.

Assassin kills Sage during the night. Sage receives no information.

Sage is poisoned when Fenrir kills them. Any pair shown is unreliable.
