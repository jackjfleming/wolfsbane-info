Assassin

Once per game, at night*, choose a player: they die, even if for some reason they could not.

Storyteller procedure

Once per game after the first night, wake Assassin and let them choose a player. The choice spends the use even if Assassin is poisoned or confused. If Assassin was functional when choosing Goon, apply Goon’s alignment change and confusion, but this newly caused confusion does not stop that Assassin attack. Kill the target despite ordinary cannot-die effects. If Assassin was already impaired before choosing, the attack has no effect.

Key interactions

The kill bypasses ordinary death protection; impairment can prevent the kill but still spends the use.

Examples

Assassin attacks protected Fool: Fool dies without consuming a survival use.
