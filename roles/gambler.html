Gambler

From night two onward, a wrong character guess causes the Gambler to die that night.

Storyteller procedure

Each night after the first, wake the Gambler. Have them identify any player, including themself, and guess that player's current character. Put the Gambler to sleep. Compare the guess with that character. If the target is a healthy Spy or Recluse and the guessed character is one they can register as, the Storyteller decides whether they register as the guessed character for this check; Game Master offers both results. If the guess is wrong and the Gambler's ability works, add the Gambler to that night's deaths, then resolve protection and announce deaths at dawn in the normal way.

Key interactions

A poisoned or confused Gambler's wrong guess does not cause this ability's death in Game Master.

The Gambler may guess their own character; a wrong self-guess can kill them just like a wrong guess about another player.

A healthy Spy may register as a Townsfolk or Outsider, and a healthy Recluse may register as a Minion or Alpha Wolf. If their ability is impaired, compare against their actual character instead.

Death protection may save a Gambler after a wrong guess; resolve the cause of death and protection in night order.

Examples

The Gambler names the Empath and guesses Empath. If that player's character is Empath, the Gambler stays alive.

The Gambler names the Fool but guesses Soldier. If the Gambler is healthy, add the Gambler to tonight's potential deaths.
