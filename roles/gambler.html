Gambler

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

From night two onward, a wrong character guess causes the Gambler to die that night.

Storyteller procedure

Each night after the first, wake the Gambler. Have them identify a player and guess that player's character; Game Master currently offers every other player as a target. Put the Gambler to sleep. Compare the guess with the target's actual character, applying any registration or impairment ruling. If the guess is wrong and the Gambler's ability works, add the Gambler to that night's deaths, then resolve protection and announce deaths at dawn in the normal way. The Game Master wizard compares the selected character name with the stored role name and proposes the death.

Key interactions

A poisoned or confused Gambler's wrong guess does not cause this ability's death in Game Master.

The current wizard excludes the Gambler from its target list even though the comparison game's rules allow a self-guess.

Death protection may save a Gambler after a wrong guess; resolve the cause of death and protection in night order.

Examples

The Gambler names the Empath and guesses Empath. If that player's character is Empath, the Gambler stays alive.

The Gambler names the Fool but guesses Soldier. If the Gambler is healthy, add the Gambler to tonight's potential deaths.

Editorial review

Decide whether the Gambler may choose themself. Game Master excludes self, while the comparison game's rules allow it.

Confirm whether registrations or character changes alter what counts as a correct guess; Game Master currently compares the stored role name directly.
