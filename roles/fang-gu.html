Fang Gu

The first Outsider your attack would kill becomes an evil Fang Gu and you die instead. This jump happens once per game; Successor stays Successor.

Storyteller procedure

Add one Outsider at setup in place of a Townsfolk. Each night except the first, wake Fang Gu to choose a player. On the first attack that would kill a player who counts as an Outsider, kill the original Fang Gu instead, make the attacked Outsider alive, evil, and the new Fang Gu, and mark the once-per-game jump as used for the rest of the game. A protected target that would not die does not cause a jump. Show the new Fang Gu their new character and evil alignment, but do not reveal the Minions to them. On later nights, Outsider attacks cause ordinary deaths. The new Fang Gu does not attack again in the same night. If Successor is in play, it remains Successor rather than creating two Alphas.

Key interactions

A player that registers as Outsider for this interaction may qualify, subject to Storyteller discretion; it is still the first qualifying jump of the game.

The Fang Gu–Scarlet Woman jinx gives the attacked Outsider the handoff instead of Successor.

If protection blocks the would-be kill, neither conversion nor original Fang Gu death occurs.

Examples

Fang Gu attacks an unprotected Outsider. Original Fang Gu dies; the Outsider becomes an evil Fang Gu and remains alive.

Fang Gu attacks a protected Outsider. The Outsider survives and no jump occurs.
