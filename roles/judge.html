Judge

Once per game, force a nomination made by another player to pass or fail, even just after its tally and before the next nomination.

Storyteller procedure

Once per game during a nomination made by someone other than Judge, or immediately after its tally before another nomination starts, Judge may force the result. A forced pass immediately executes the nominee regardless of tally, then resolve survival and victory rules; Butcher may still gain its extra nomination. A forced fail cancels this nomination’s votes and restores any earlier leading nomination as appropriate. The choice spends Judge’s use even if Judge is impaired.

Key interactions

The restriction is on who nominated, not whom they nominated.

An execution can occur without death; the relevant roles check their own trigger wording.

Examples

Judge forces another player’s nomination to pass with zero votes. The nominee is executed, though protection may keep them alive.
