Tea Lady

If both nearest living neighbors register as good, Tea Lady prevents either from dying while her ability works.

Storyteller procedure

Continually identify Tea Lady’s nearest living neighbor in each direction, skipping dead players. Good Outsiders and good Travelers count as good neighbors. At the Storyteller’s discretion, Spy or Recluse registration may change whether a neighbor counts as good for this check. If both neighbors count as good and Tea Lady has a working ability, neither neighbor can die, including by execution or exile; Assassin is an exception. Tea Lady does not protect themself. Recheck whenever deaths, alignment, registration, or impairment changes the condition. An execution or exile still happens even when the protected player survives.

Key interactions

Good Outsiders and good Travelers may satisfy the two-good-neighbors condition.

Spy and Recluse may register as good or evil for this check at the Storyteller’s discretion. If either neighbor counts as evil, neither is protected.

A protected Traveler can be exiled but does not die; exile still occurs.

A poisoned or confused Tea Lady protects neither neighbor. Assassin can bypass Tea Lady’s no-death protection.

Examples

Tea Lady has a good Outsider and a good Townsfolk as nearest living neighbors. Both are protected.

One living neighbor turns evil. Neither neighbor remains protected.

A protected neighbor is executed but survives; no more nominations happen that day.

A protected good Traveler is exiled. The exile occurs, but Tea Lady prevents the Traveler from dying.
