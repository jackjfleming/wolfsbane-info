Tea Lady

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

If both closest living neighbors are good, Tea Lady prevents either neighbor from dying while her ability works.

Storyteller procedure

Continually identify Tea Lady’s nearest living neighbor in each direction, skipping dead players. If both neighbors are currently good and Tea Lady has a working ability, neither neighbor can die from ordinary death attempts, including execution; Assassin is an exception. Tea Lady does not protect themself. Recheck the condition whenever deaths, alignment, or impairment changes the situation. If a protected neighbor is executed, announce that they were executed but did not die; the day’s execution is still used.

Key interactions

A good Outsider is a Villager and can satisfy the condition. Confirm whether a good Traveler does too.

An evil neighbor means neither neighbor is protected. A poisoned or confused Tea Lady protects neither.

In the comparison game, a protected Traveler may be exiled yet survive. Confirm whether Wolf’s Bane follows that ruling; Game Master currently applies exile as a death.

Spy and Recluse registration may affect whether a neighbor counts as good; confirm the Storyteller choice.

Assassin can kill a protected neighbor despite Tea Lady’s no-death effect.

Examples

Tea Lady has a good Outsider and a good Townsfolk as nearest living neighbors. Both are protected.

One living neighbor turns evil. Neither neighbor remains protected.

A protected neighbor is executed but survives; no more nominations happen that day.

Editorial review

Confirm whether good Travelers count as Villagers for Tea Lady, whether Spy/Recluse registration can change the condition, and whether a protected Traveler survives exile.

Game Master checks actual alignment and applies exile directly without Tea Lady protection.
