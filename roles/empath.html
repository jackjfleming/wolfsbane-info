Empath

Each night, learn how many of your 2 living neighbors are Werewolves.

Storyteller procedure

Each night, wake the Empath. On each side of the Empath, skip dead players to find the nearest living neighbor. Count how many of these two living neighbors register as Werewolves, and show the Empath 0, 1, or 2 fingers. The Recluse may register as a Werewolf for this information. Put the Empath to sleep.

Key interactions

A Recluse may register as a Werewolf neighbor or not, at the Storyteller's discretion.

A Werewolf-aligned Traveler counts as a Werewolf neighbor when alive.

The Empath's information can be false while the Empath is poisoned or confused.

Examples

The Empath neighbors two good players—a Soldier and a Bodyguard. The Empath learns a '0'.

The next day, the Soldier is executed. That night, the Bodyguard is killed by the Alpha Wolf. The Empath now detects the players sitting next to the Soldier and the Bodyguard, which are a Librarian and an evil Gunslinger. The Empath now learns a '1'.

There are only three players left alive: the Empath, the Alpha Wolf, and the Baron. No matter who is seated where, the Empath learns a '2'.

Tips & tricks

You get information based on the state of the game when you wake up (after the Alpha Wolf has killed that night). This means that if one of your neighbors died in the night, you immediately start to learn about the next living player.

The information you receive should inform how you handle your neighbors. If you get a "0", you are (probably) sitting between two good players who you can coordinate with and might want to try to keep alive. If you get a count of "1", then one of your neighbors is trustworthy, and one is not. You should try to determine which one of your neighbors you trust, and eliminate the other one. If you get a count of "2", then you are sitting between two evil players, and should probably look to change that as soon as possible.

If you trust your neighbors, keeping them alive drastically narrows the list of Alpha Wolf suspects.

If your neighbors die, you will receive new information on the next nearest living player to you. Getting your neighbors killed means you know more. Coordinating with a Gravedigger to confirm the identities of your neighbors and getting new information down the line is a powerful combo. Even if your neighbors are both good, it can be worth executing a Hunter who has used their ability so that you know if you can trust the information from the Fortune Teller the other side of them.

Coming out with your information early (especially if you know you're sitting near evil players) can help you coordinate with the rest of the good team to further check out your suspects.

Waiting to reveal your information means that you can protect your good neighbors or keep an eye on your evil ones, as well as gather a wider portfolio of information.

If you get a count of "1" or "2", consider telling your neighbors you got a count of "0" instead. This will make you appear to be the Village Idiot or poisoned, and the evil team may be more inclined to keep you alive since you do not appear to be a threat. You could also try claiming to be the Soldier or another character the Alpha Wolf doesn't want to attack. Remember to come out with your real information before the end of the game!

Travelers who neighbor you count for your information. If you're suddenly getting a "1" when previously you were getting "0", look to the newly arrived Traveler with some suspicion.

Beware the Spy! They may register as good for you, and give you an incorrect count. Additionally, the Recluse may register as evil, making you believe you are sitting next to an evil player when that is not actually the case.
