Sailor

Each night, choose a living player; the Storyteller confuses either that player or Sailor until dusk. A healthy Sailor cannot die.

Storyteller procedure

Each night, wake Sailor and ask them to choose any living player, including themself. If they point to a dead player, ask them to choose again. If Sailor’s ability is working, decide whether Sailor or the chosen player becomes confused until the following dusk; choosing themself necessarily confuses Sailor. If Sailor is already impaired, the choice creates no new confusion. Mark the effect and clear it at dusk. While Sailor has a working ability and is not confused, they cannot die, including by execution. If they are executed but survive, announce the execution and end nominations for the day.

Key interactions

A healthy Sailor cannot die even before they first wake to choose.

If Sailor later becomes poisoned or confused by another source, confusion they caused to another player pauses; it resumes if Sailor recovers before that effect would expire. An action attempted while already impaired creates no delayed effect.

Droison-cycle rule: if effects form a closed chain that would cancel itself, every player in that loop stays poisoned or confused and has no working ability. The loop remains impaired until one effect reaches its natural expiration. Then resolve the remaining effects in ordinary order. Sailor choosing themself is a one-player loop and remains confused until dusk.

Assassin can kill through Sailor’s no-death protection.

Examples

Sailor chooses a living Empath. The Storyteller confuses Empath until dusk; Sailor survives an Alpha attack that night.

Sailor chooses themself. Sailor is confused and can die until the effect clears.

A healthy Sailor is executed and remains alive; that still uses the day’s execution.

Sailor confuses themself. This self-cycle remains in effect until dusk; Sailor can die while confused.

Player A poisons Player B; B confuses Player C; C poisons A. All three remain impaired until one effect expires, then the remaining effects resolve in normal order.
