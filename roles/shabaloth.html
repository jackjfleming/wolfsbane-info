Shabaloth

After the first night choose two attack targets in order. Before a later Shabaloth wake, Storyteller may revive one player attacked the previous night, including someone already dead when attacked.

Storyteller procedure

Each night except the first, wake Shabaloth to choose two players one after another; resolve attacks in that order. Before each later Shabaloth wake, Storyteller may revive one dead player Shabaloth chose on the previous night, including someone already dead when chosen. A revived player regains their ability and a used once-per-game ability; a first-night-only ability runs on that revival night; another wake ability uses its ordinary slot, waiting until the following night if that slot has passed. Announce revival at dawn. Shabaloth cannot revive themself after their own death because their ability is no longer active.

Key interactions

Self, repeat, and dead targets may be chosen, but an already dead target does not die again.

Revival happens before Shabaloth acts and rechecks ordinary wake order.

Examples

Shabaloth attacks a dead Professor and a living Townsfolk. On the next night Storyteller may revive Professor before Shabaloth wakes.
