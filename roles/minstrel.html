Minstrel

When a Minion dies by execution, everyone else except Travelers is confused until the following dusk.

Storyteller procedure

When a living Minion actually dies by execution while the Minstrel has their ability, mark every other player except Travelers as confused immediately. This includes dead players and the Alpha Wolf, but not the Minstrel. Keep the effect through tonight and tomorrow, then remove it at the following dusk. A Minion killed another way, an already-dead Minion who is executed, or an execution that does not kill does not trigger it. If another Minion dies by execution while the ability works, renew the effect through the next dusk.

Key interactions

The Minstrel must have a working ability when the Minion dies; a poisoned or confused Minstrel does not trigger it.

An immediate execution, such as New in Town, can trigger Minstrel if a Minion actually dies. Exile is not execution.

Confused players keep acting when instructed, but their abilities have no effect while the Minstrel effect lasts. Travelers are excluded.

Examples

The town executes a Minion who dies. The Alpha Wolf and all other non-Travelers except the Minstrel are confused that night and through the next day.

A Minion is executed but protected from death. The Minstrel effect does not begin.
