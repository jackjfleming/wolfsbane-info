Klutz

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

When Klutz learns they died, they publicly choose one living player; if that player is evil, Klutz’s current team loses.

Storyteller procedure

When Klutz learns that they died, allow a short discussion, then have them publicly name one living player. If that player counts as evil for this check and Klutz has a working ability, Klutz’s current team loses immediately; otherwise the game continues. A dead target is invalid and must be replaced. If Klutz is evil and chooses an evil player, the evil team loses. If Klutz is poisoned or confused, the choice has no effect. Privately remind a new player to make the choice if needed. Resolve any Bill inversion after determining the underlying winner.

Key interactions

Confirm whether “Werewolf” in the printed ability means current evil alignment, including an evil Goon or evil Traveler.

Spy and Recluse registration might change whether the chosen player counts as evil; confirm Storyteller discretion.

The absence of a loss does not prove the target is good: impairment, registration, or a false Klutz claim may explain it.

Examples

A good Klutz dies at night, learns this at dawn, and publicly chooses a living evil Goon. If evil alignment qualifies, good loses.

An evil Klutz publicly chooses an evil player. Klutz’s own evil team loses.

Klutz chooses a good player while Bill’s ability works. Klutz causes no win or loss; play continues.

Tips & tricks

Plan a trusted living choice before you die, but remember that registration and impairment can complicate what the result proves.

The choice must be public and made after you learn of your death.

Editorial review

Confirm that “Werewolf” means current evil alignment, including evil Goon and evil Travelers, rather than only Werewolf character types.

Confirm Spy/Recluse registration and how Bill applies to a Klutz-triggered loss. Game Master currently broadcasts a reminder but does not resolve Klutz’s choice.
