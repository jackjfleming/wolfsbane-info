Klutz

When Klutz learns they died, they publicly choose one living player; if that player counts as a Werewolf, Klutz’s current team loses.

Storyteller procedure

When Klutz learns that they died, allow a short discussion, then have them publicly name one living player. If that player counts as a Werewolf for this check and Klutz has a working ability, Klutz’s current team loses immediately; otherwise the game continues. Use the target’s current team, including a Werewolf Goon or Traveler, subject to Spy and Recluse registration at Storyteller discretion. A dead target is invalid and must be replaced. If Klutz is a Werewolf and chooses a player who counts as a Werewolf, the Werewolf team loses. If Klutz is poisoned or confused, the choice has no effect. Privately remind a new player to make the choice if needed. Resolve any Bill inversion after determining the underlying winner.

Key interactions

“Werewolf” means the target counts as a member of the Werewolf team at the time of the choice, including a Werewolf Goon or Traveler.

Spy and Recluse may register differently for this check at Storyteller discretion.

The absence of a loss does not prove the target is a Villager: impairment, registration, or a false Klutz claim may explain it.

Examples

A Villager Klutz dies at night, learns this at dawn, and publicly chooses a living Werewolf Goon. The Villager team loses.

A Werewolf Klutz publicly chooses a player who counts as a Werewolf. The Werewolf team loses.

Klutz chooses a Villager while Bill’s ability works. Klutz causes no win or loss; play continues.

Tips & tricks

Plan a trusted living choice before you die, but remember that registration and impairment can complicate what the result proves.

The choice must be public and made after you learn of your death.
