Soldier

A healthy Soldier is safe from harmful effects of the Alpha’s ability, including its kill.

Storyteller procedure

When an Alpha ability would kill a Soldier whose ability is working, leave Soldier alive. Do not let another harmful effect of that same Alpha ability affect them, such as poisoning or turning evil. The Alpha does not choose a replacement target just because the attack failed. Soldier can still die by execution or by abilities from other characters. If Soldier is poisoned or confused, resolve the Alpha effect normally.

Key interactions

Protection is from the Alpha’s ability, not every night death or action taken by the Alpha player.

A poisoned Soldier has no protection; a Soldier who is actually Village Idiot has no Soldier ability.

Other protection may also explain a night without deaths, so a zero-death night does not prove Soldier was attacked.

Examples

Fenrir attacks a healthy Soldier. Soldier remains alive.

Poisoner poisons Soldier before Fenrir attacks. Soldier dies.

Gossip kills a healthy Soldier at night. Soldier can die because Gossip is not an Alpha ability.

Tips & tricks

A night without deaths does not prove that Soldier was attacked; several abilities can prevent or redirect a kill.

Soldier is still vulnerable to execution, non-Alpha deaths, and impairment.

A Soldier dying at night does not identify the cause by itself.
