Zombuul

If nobody died today, attack tonight. The first time you would die, you appear dead but remain secretly alive; while apparently dead, use ordinary dead-player public rules.

Storyteller procedure

On the first would-be death while Zombuul has its ability, do not actually kill Zombuul, but announce a death, flip their public life marker, and mark them as apparently dead. This counts as a death today for Zombuul’s wake condition. They have one public dead vote token, cannot nominate, and are skipped as a living neighbor, but remain secretly alive for their own ability and victory. A second would-be death kills them for real. If nobody died today, wake Zombuul after the first night to choose one player to attack. Executing an already dead player without a new death does not block this wake. If Zombuul is impaired before their first would-be death, the first-death protection does not work; do not announce an apparently dead Zombuul as alive merely because it later becomes impaired.

Key interactions

An apparent first death is not a real Alpha death for Mastermind or Successor, although it counts as a public death and blocks that night’s Zombuul attack.

A truly dead Zombuul ends the game under normal Alpha-death rules, subject to other active effects.

Examples

Zombuul is executed and appears dead. It does not attack that night; on a later no-death day, it may attack while still publicly dead.
