Courtier

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

Once per game at night, name a character; that character is poisoned for three nights and three days.

Storyteller procedure

On a night when the Courtier has not used their ability, wake them and ask them to choose a character name, including one that may not be in play. Put the Courtier to sleep and mark the choice as used. If that character is in play and the Courtier's ability works, mark its player poisoned for this night and the next two nights, with the intervening three days. Game Master applies a 'Courtier Poisoned: 3' tag to a matching player and reduces the counter at transitions into later nights. Check the tag and other causes of impairment before resolving that character's actions; clear the effect when its duration ends.

Key interactions

The Courtier chooses a character, not a player. A choice that is absent from the game still spends the once-per-game use.

The chosen character's ability stops working while the Courtier effect applies, but the player may still wake and make choices as usual.

Game Master uses the same poisoned state for several causes of impairment. Check whether another cause remains when the Courtier counter ends.

Examples

The Courtier names Poisoner and that character is in play. Mark the Poisoner affected for the current night and the following two nights and days.

The Courtier names a character that is not in play. Record the use, but no player receives the effect.

Editorial review

Decide whether Wolf’s Bane intentionally says 'poisoned' rather than the source game's 'drunk'; if so, confirm whether poison-specific interactions apply.

Confirm what happens if the Courtier is impaired when choosing or becomes impaired during the three-night effect. Game Master currently only applies the tag when the Courtier is healthy and alive at selection.
