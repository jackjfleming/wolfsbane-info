Courtier

Once per game at night, name a character; that character is poisoned for three nights and three days.

Storyteller procedure

On a night when the Courtier has not used their ability, wake them and ask them to choose a character name, including one that may not be in play. Put the Courtier to sleep and mark the choice as used, even if the Courtier is poisoned. If the Courtier is healthy and that character is in play, mark its player poisoned for this night and the next two nights, with the intervening three days. A poisoned Courtier's choice has no effect and does not begin later if the Courtier recovers. If the Courtier becomes poisoned after a successful choice, the chosen player temporarily recovers; if the Courtier recovers before the duration ends, the chosen player is poisoned again. Keep counting the three-night duration while the effect is paused. Game Master records the duration separately from the active poison and clears both at expiry.

Key interactions

The Courtier chooses a character, not a player. A choice that is absent from the game still spends the once-per-game use.

The chosen character's ability stops working while the Courtier effect applies, but the player may still wake and make choices as usual.

Other poison or confusion can remain when the Courtier effect pauses or ends. Check each source separately.

Examples

The Courtier names Poisoner and that character is in play. Mark the Poisoner affected for the current night and the following two nights and days.

The Courtier names a character that is not in play. Record the use, but no player receives the effect.
