Lunatic

You think you are an Alpha. Your apparent choices do nothing; the real Alpha knows who you are and sees every player you choose each night.

Storyteller procedure

Wake Lunatic on the first night as an apparent Alpha: show an apparent Minion team and three bluffs, which may be false or in play. Show the real Alpha that they are the real Alpha and identify Lunatic. Each attack night, wake Lunatic first as the Alpha type they believe they are, record every chosen target, then show those targets to the real Alpha before the Alpha acts. Lunatic choices have no kill effect. A different apparent Alpha type may be used, including one that chooses multiple targets.

Key interactions

The real Alpha sees every player Lunatic targeted, including multiple targets; the Alpha still makes their own choice.

Examples

The apparent Alpha and real Alpha may choose different targets, so a different player dies from the one Lunatic chose.
