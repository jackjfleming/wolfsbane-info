Trapper

After the first night, choose a player different from last night. If they are the Alpha, reveal Trapper to that Alpha and skip its ordinary wake tonight.

Storyteller procedure

Each night after the first, wake Trapper and ask them to choose any player, including themself or a dead player, except the player they chose last night. Record the choice even if Trapper is poisoned or confused; it still restricts tomorrow's choice. If Trapper’s ability works and the chosen player is the actual Alpha, wake that Alpha, identify Trapper, then do not wake the Alpha for its ordinary action tonight. Give Trapper no direct result. Other reasons for the Alpha to wake and already-running or passive Alpha effects still resolve. If Trapper is impaired, the Alpha is not blocked or told who Trapper is. A healthy Recluse may register as Alpha for the reveal; the actual Alpha still wakes normally in that case.

Key interactions

Trapper no longer protects a chosen player; Bodyguard is Wolf’s Bane’s Monk counterpart and supplies that protection.

An Exorcist-style block suppresses the Alpha's ordinary wake and new action, not a Pukka delayed death, a Zombuul survival ability, a Shabaloth regurgitation, or a wake caused by another character.

The actual Alpha learns Trapper’s identity when chosen. Trapper receives no yes/no result, even after choosing the Alpha.

A healthy Recluse may register as Alpha and receive Trapper’s identity without stopping the actual Alpha. Spy cannot register as Alpha. A notification wake does not count as an Alpha ability wake for Chambermaid.

Po retains an existing charge when blocked; the skipped wake neither charges nor spends it. Warden already has a similar Wolf’s Bane ability.

Examples

Trapper chooses Fenrir. Fenrir privately learns who Trapper is and does not wake for its ordinary attack that night; Trapper is not told that the choice succeeded.

Trapper chooses Pukka after Pukka poisoned a player yesterday. Pukka skips choosing a new target tonight, but the previously poisoned player's delayed death can still occur.
