Matron

Once each day, swap up to three pairs of seats consecutively. The new order persists; private game talks are limited to immediate seated neighbors.

Storyteller procedure

Once each day, Matron may choose up to three pairs of players to swap seats, one pair after another in a single opportunity. The same player may move more than once. Update the Grimoire and recompute seating-dependent abilities after each swap; the final arrangement persists until changed. While Matron’s ability works, players may discuss the game privately only with immediate seated neighbors and may not leave their seats to do so. Make reasonable accessibility accommodations for anyone who cannot move seats.

Key interactions

Seat changes immediately update Empath, Tea Lady, No Dashii, and other seat-dependent effects.

The private-talk restriction ends if Matron dies or is impaired; seating changes already made remain.

Examples

Matron swaps two pairs and stops. They cannot return later that same day for a third swap.
