Moonchild

When you learn you died, publicly choose a living player. If they counted as a Villager then, they die tonight if your ability works and protection does not save them.

Storyteller procedure

When Moonchild learns of their death, ask them to publicly choose one living player. Determine at that choice whether the player counts as a Villager, including a good Outsider or good Traveler and discretionary Spy/Recluse registration. Record that result. Tonight, if Moonchild is able to act, attempt to kill that player only if the recorded result was Villager; resolve the target’s applicable protection normally.

Key interactions

Check alignment and registration when Moonchild chooses, not when the night kill resolves. Protection can prevent the death according to the protection’s scope.

Examples

Moonchild selects a good Traveler, who later changes alignment. The saved Villager check still qualifies, but a protection that covers the kill may save them.
