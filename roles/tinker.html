Tinker

While your ability works, the Storyteller may choose to kill you at any time. Protection against death can prevent it; protection limited to Alpha effects cannot.

Storyteller procedure

While Tinker is alive and their ability works, Storyteller may choose a moment during day or night to attempt a Tinker death. Check protection at that moment: general death protection can prevent it, but Alpha-only protection cannot because the source is Tinker. Announce a successful day death immediately or a night death at dawn. The Storyteller chooses its timing relative to other actions and final-day win checks. A successful death follows ordinary death rules: it triggers another ability only if that ability’s own cause and timing conditions are met.

Key interactions

A daytime Tinker death counts as a death today, so Zombuul does not attack that night.

A Tinker death is not an Alpha kill and does not trigger Grandmother merely because the Grandchild died.

A prevented death triggers no death-dependent ability.

Examples

Storyteller kills Tinker during the day. Zombuul sees a death today and does not attack tonight.

A good Tinker seated next to a healthy Tea Lady cannot die while that protection works.
