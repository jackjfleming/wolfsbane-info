Tinker

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

While your ability works, the Storyteller may choose to kill you at any time. Protection against death can prevent it; protection limited to Alpha effects cannot.

Storyteller procedure

While Tinker is alive and their ability works, the Storyteller may choose a moment during the day or night for Tinker to die. Check protection at that moment. Protection from death can prevent this death; protection limited to Alpha effects cannot, because Tinker is dying from their own ability. Announce a successful day death immediately or record a night death and announce it at dawn. The Storyteller chooses when to attempt the death relative to other actions and a final-day win check; resolve any win condition using the actual living players at the time it is checked.

Key interactions

Tinker’s ability is the death source, not an Alpha attack. General death protection can save Tinker; Alpha-only protection cannot.

A prevented death causes no death-triggered abilities to fire. The remaining question is whether a successful Tinker death follows all ordinary death-trigger rules without exception.

Examples

Tinker is protected only from Alpha effects. The Storyteller chooses to kill Tinker during the day; Tinker dies and the death is announced immediately.

Tinker has protection from death. The Storyteller attempts to kill Tinker through their ability; the protection prevents the death.

Editorial review

Owner confirmed free day/night timing, that Alpha-only protection does not stop Tinker’s own ability, that other death protection may stop it, and that the Storyteller chooses the timing relative to final-day win checks. Confirm whether a successful Tinker death triggers other roles exactly as an ordinary death before approving the guide.
