Bone Collector

Once per game at night, restore a dead player’s ability until dusk, including spent once-per-game or first-night abilities.

Storyteller procedure

On a night after the first, Bone Collector may choose one dead player. The choice spends Bone Collector’s use. While Bone Collector is alive and able to act, the dead player regains their ability until the next dusk while remaining dead. A spent once-per-game ability is available again during this window; a first-night-only ability may run this night. End the borrowed ability at dusk or earlier if Bone Collector dies or loses the ability.

Key interactions

The chosen player remains dead for voting, nomination, and neighbor checks.

If Bone Collector is impaired, an attempted choice spends the once-per-game use without restoring an ability, following the approved impaired-use rule.

Examples

Bone Collector restores a dead Artist; Artist may ask one question before dusk while still dead.
