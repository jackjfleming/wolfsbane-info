Bill

When Bill’s ability works at game end, reverse the winner and loser, even if Bill is dead.

Storyteller procedure

At setup, allow only one Bill ability in play. Bill cannot coexist with Baron, Godfather, Spy, Lleech, Pit-Hag, or Widow; the latter three characters are not currently selectable in Wolf’s Bane. If Boffin is ever added, its Demon cannot gain Bill’s ability. Do not create a second Bill ability through a character change or ability copy. When any victory condition would end the game, first determine which team would win without Bill. If Bill has a working ability at that moment, declare the opposite team the winner. Bill continues to have this ability while dead, but poisoning or confusion suspends it. Apply this to ordinary wins and ability-caused wins alike, including Saint, Klutz, and Mayor. Resolve the final winner once.

Key interactions

Bill’s death does not turn off the inversion. Check Bill’s impairment at the moment the game ends.

A poisoned or confused Bill does not invert the result while impaired; if Bill recovers before game end, the inversion applies again.

Heretic jinxes apply to Bill: only one of Bill and Baron, Godfather, Spy, Lleech, Pit-Hag, or Widow may be in play. Boffin’s Demon may not gain Bill’s ability if Boffin is added.

Only one Bill ability may exist at a time, including after a character change or ability copy.

Examples

Fenrir is executed. Good would normally win, so a working Bill makes evil win.

Saint is executed. Evil would normally win, so a working Bill makes good win.

Bill is dead but healthy when a victory condition occurs. The outcome is still reversed.
