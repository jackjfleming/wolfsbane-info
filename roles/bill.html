Bill

Draft guidance — pending Wolf's Bane review

When Bill’s ability works at game end, reverse the winner and loser, even if Bill is dead.

Storyteller procedure

When any victory condition would end the game, first determine which team would win without Bill. If Bill has a working ability at that moment, declare the opposite team the winner. Bill continues to have this ability while dead, but poisoning or confusion suspends it. Apply this to ordinary wins and ability-caused wins alike, including Saint, Klutz, and Mayor. Resolve the final winner once; do not invert an already-inverted announcement again.

Key interactions

Bill’s death does not turn off the inversion. Check Bill’s impairment at the moment the game ends.

A poisoned or confused Bill does not invert the result while impaired; if Bill recovers before game end, the inversion applies again.

Confirm whether any setup incompatibilities from the comparison game’s Heretic should apply to Wolf’s Bane, and how multiple copies of Bill’s ability would interact.

Examples

Fenrir is executed. Good would normally win, so a working Bill makes evil win.

Saint is executed. Evil would normally win, so a working Bill makes good win.

Bill is dead but healthy when a victory condition occurs. The outcome is still reversed.

Editorial review

Confirm whether Wolf’s Bane adopts Heretic-style setup incompatibilities, especially Spy, Baron, and Godfather.

Confirm how simultaneous Bill abilities would interact if Apprentice can gain Bill.
