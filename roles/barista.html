Barista

Storyteller chooses both recipient and effect nightly. The recipient learns whether they have true information and sobriety, or their ability works twice until dusk.

Storyteller procedure

Each night Storyteller chooses any player and one Barista effect. Privately tell that player, not Barista, which effect applies. Until dusk, either make them sober and healthy and guarantee true information despite ordinary false-information sources and registration, or let their ability work twice at its normal timing. An unused once-per-game ability may be used twice during the effect; an already-spent one may be used once more. Mandatory effects happen twice; optional abilities may be used twice. Clear the effect at dusk.

Key interactions

True-information mode suppresses drunkenness and poisoning for its duration and guarantees true information even when Spy or Recluse registration would otherwise mislead.

The chosen recipient may be a Werewolf; Barista does not know whom Storyteller chose.

Examples

Storyteller chooses a poisoned Empath for true-information mode. Empath is healthy and learns true information until dusk.

Storyteller chooses an Artist who already asked their question; Artist may ask once more before dusk.
