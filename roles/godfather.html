minion

Godfather

You start knowing which Outsiders are in play. If 1 died today, choose a player tonight: they die. [-1 or +1 Outsider]

Storyteller procedure

At setup choose either one additional Outsider or one fewer Outsider, within the available script, and do not combine Godfather with Bill. Tell Godfather which Outsider characters are in play. If at least one player who counts as an Outsider dies during the day, including by execution or exile, wake Godfather that night to choose exactly one player to kill. A night death does not qualify.

Key interactions

Discretionary registration can make a dead Spy or Recluse count as Outsider for this trigger. Multiple qualifying deaths still grant one kill.

Examples

Two Outsiders die during the day; Godfather chooses one night victim.
