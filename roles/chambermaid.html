Chambermaid

Count how many of the two chosen living players woke tonight for their own abilities; give 0, 1, or 2.

Storyteller procedure

Each night, wake the Chambermaid and have them choose two different living players other than themself. Determine whether each chosen player woke that night because of their own character ability, including a wake while that ability was poisoned or confused. Do not count a wake caused only by another character or a general game event. Show the total on your fingers, then put the Chambermaid to sleep. Game Master suggests a count, but enter the result yourself after checking exceptional wakes and impairments.

Key interactions

A poisoned or confused chosen player can still count if they actually woke for their own ability; impairment affects the ability's effect, not whether the wake happened.

If the Chambermaid is poisoned or confused, the information may be false. Game Master offers a decoy count but leaves the final number to the Storyteller.

The two choices must be distinct, alive, and neither can be the Chambermaid.

Examples

The Chambermaid chooses the alive Empath and Fool. The Empath woke for their own ability and the Fool did not, so show 1.

Both chosen players woke for their own abilities, but one was poisoned. Both wakes count, so show 2 if the Chambermaid is healthy.
