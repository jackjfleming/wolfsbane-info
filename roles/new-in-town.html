New in Town

The first time New in Town is nominated, a qualifying Townsfolk nominator is immediately executed.

Storyteller procedure

On the first valid nomination of New in Town, spend the ability even if New in Town is poisoned or the nominator does not qualify. If New in Town has a working ability and the nominator is a Townsfolk (or qualifies through registration), immediately execute the nominator, resolve whether they die, end nominations, and proceed to night. The execution counts for the day even when another ability prevents death. Otherwise continue the nomination and vote normally. Later nominations do not trigger New in Town again.

Key interactions

For this ability, a good Outsider is a Villager but is not a Townsfolk nominator. A Spy may register as Townsfolk and trigger the execution.

A poisoned New in Town spends the first nomination without executing the nominator.

If an executed nominator survives through protection, the execution still happened and the day ends.

Examples

A healthy Housekeeper first nominates New in Town. Housekeeper is immediately executed; if they die, mark their death and end nominations.

A good Outsider first nominates New in Town. No immediate execution occurs, and the ability is spent.

A Spy first nominates New in Town and registers as Townsfolk. The Spy is immediately executed; any applicable death protection is then resolved.

Tips & tricks

A Townsfolk nominating a healthy New in Town is immediately executed. This can support both players’ claims, but Spy registration and impairment prevent absolute confirmation.

Invite a Townsfolk who has already used a one-time ability to nominate you when the town can afford the execution.

A good Outsider is a Villager but does not qualify as a Townsfolk nominator for this ability. The first nomination still spends your ability.

You can nominate yourself if you are a Townsfolk. The resulting execution ends nominations for the day, even if another ability prevents your death.

A missing immediate execution may mean the nominator was not a Townsfolk, you were impaired, or you are not actually New in Town.
