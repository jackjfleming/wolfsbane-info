Seer

Each night, Seer chooses two distinct players and learns whether either is an Alpha or the fixed good red herring.

Storyteller procedure

At setup, mark one good player as Seer’s red herring; this can be Seer themself and does not move during the game. Each night, wake Seer to choose two distinct players, living or dead. Give a yes if either is an Alpha or the red herring, subject to registration; otherwise give a no. Do not reveal which target caused a yes. If Seer is poisoned or confused, the answer may be unreliable.

Key interactions

A dead Alpha can still produce a yes. If the Alpha identity moves to a different player, later reads follow the current characters.

Recluse may register as an Alpha and add another possible yes beyond the fixed red herring.

A yes does not prove either target is the Alpha; a no is only reliable while Seer’s ability is working.

Examples

Seer chooses the current Alpha and an Empath and receives yes.

Seer chooses themself and the fixed red herring and receives yes, even though neither is the Alpha.

Seer chooses a dead Alpha and a living Butler and receives yes.

Tips & tricks

Track the same red herring throughout the game. A yes can also come from Recluse registration or impairment.

Seer may choose themself and dead players, but must choose two distinct players.

A prior no does not rule out a player who later becomes the Alpha.
